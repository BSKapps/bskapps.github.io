About

Native macOS apps from Sydney, Australia.

Who we are

BSK Apps builds native macOS utilities inspired by common problems seen in the AV world that needed proper solutions. Every app starts as a real tool used on real shows, then gets shaped into something other operators can pick up and run with.

We are based in Sydney, Australia, and build exclusively for macOS.

What we make

Small, focused apps that do one job well. No subscriptions for our paid apps, no analytics, no accounts. You buy a licence, you own that version, and updates within the major version are included.

Current line-up includes Quicker IP (network utilities) and Lab Assistant (QLab workspace automation). Free Reaper scripts are available on the resources page.

How we build

Every app is a native Swift build, signed and notarised by Apple, and distributed through either the Mac App Store or our own installer. No Electron, no cross-platform wrappers, no third-party trackers.
