Refund Policy

Last updated: 6 April 2026

Overview

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you experience a genuine issue with our software, we will work with you to resolve it.

Eligible Refunds

Refunds may be granted within 14 days of purchase if:

  • The software does not function as described and the issue cannot be resolved
  • A critical bug prevents you from using the software and no fix can be provided
  • The software is incompatible with your system despite meeting the stated requirements

Not Eligible for Refund

Refunds are not provided for:

  • Change of mind or no longer needing the software
  • Failure to read the product description or feature list before purchasing
  • Issues caused by unsupported system configurations or third-party software
  • Requests made more than 14 days after purchase

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email support@bskapps.com with your order number and a description of the issue. We will respond within 48 hours.

Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy and returned to your original payment method. Processing times depend on your bank or card issuer.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@bskapps.com.
