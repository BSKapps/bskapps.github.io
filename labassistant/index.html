Lab Assistant

QLab workspace automation for macOS

macOS Swift / SwiftUI

A companion tool for QLab that takes the grind out of show preparation. Lab Assistant automates the repetitive tasks so you can focus on the creative work. Works with both free and licensed QLab workspaces.

Features

  • Auto-set levels across your workspace
  • Trim start and end silence from audio cues automatically
  • Colour-code cues for quick visual organisation
  • Convert mono files to dual mono with crosspoint routing
  • Batch processing — set up entire shows in minutes
  • Works with QLab 4 and QLab 5

Who It's For

  • Sound designers building shows from scratch
  • AV technicians prepping workspaces for events
  • Theatre and live production crews on tight timelines
  • Anyone who's spent too long manually setting cue levels
