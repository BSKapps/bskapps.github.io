Fetch Puppy

Find and save music and video for your projects

macOS 14.0+ Coming Soon

Built for presentations, lectures, and research. Find videos to embed in slide decks, source walk-up or event music that needs trimming and conversion, or build a local reference library from a Spotify or Apple Music playlist. Fetch Puppy is a search and retrieval tool for macOS.

Features

  • Multi-source search with smart quality scoring
  • Preview search results before saving
  • Drag in a Spotify or Apple Music playlist - find every track automatically
  • Find and save music videos for an entire Spotify or Apple Music playlist
  • Save videos from TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, Reddit and more
  • Audio conversion to multiple formats with full metadata and artwork
  • Background processing with menu bar progress
