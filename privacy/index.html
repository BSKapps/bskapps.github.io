Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Overview

BSK Apps builds native macOS applications. We respect your privacy and design our apps to work without collecting, storing, or transmitting your personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or receive any personal data. Our apps have:

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No BSK Apps servers - we do not operate any

License Activation (paid apps)

Apps purchased through LemonSqueezy send your license key to LemonSqueezy's API to activate and periodically re-validate it. No other personal information is sent. LemonSqueezy's own privacy policy applies to your purchase details.

Software Updates

Some apps sold outside the App Store check GitHub for updates using the Sparkle framework. These checks send no personal data.

Fetch Puppy

Fetch Puppy downloads media on your behalf, so it talks directly to the services you use it with:

  • Requests go to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and the social media sites you download from. These services see the requests the same way a web browser would.
  • To access sites that require a login, Fetch Puppy can read cookies from your web browser (Firefox, Chrome, Brave, or Edge) and pass them only to the site they belong to. Cookies are never sent to BSK Apps or anyone else.
  • Search suggestions are fetched from Google as you type in the Search tab.
  • It downloads its yt-dlp engine and updates from GitHub.
  • Optional Spotify API credentials and your license key are stored in the macOS Keychain. Download history stays on your Mac.

Quicker IP

Quicker IP makes a single HTTPS request to a third-party service (such as ifconfig.me) to display your public IP address. No personal data is sent with this request. This feature can be disabled in Settings. All other network information shown (IP addresses, adapter details, scan results) is read from your local system and never transmitted.

Location Access

Quicker IP Pro may request location permission solely to display your Wi-Fi network name (SSID), as required by macOS. Location data is not stored, transmitted, or used for any other purpose. This feature is optional and can be disabled in Settings.

Local Storage

Our apps store preferences and settings locally on your Mac using standard macOS mechanisms (UserDefaults and the Keychain). This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect any data from any user, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@bskapps.com.
