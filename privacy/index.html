Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 April 2026

Overview

BSK Apps builds native macOS applications. We respect your privacy and design our apps to work without collecting, storing, or transmitting your personal data.

Data Collection

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No data sent to our servers
  • No cookies or identifiers
  • No access to contacts, calendars, or files outside the app

Network Access

Some of our apps access the network for core functionality:

  • Quicker IP makes a single HTTPS request to a third-party service (such as ifconfig.me) to display your public IP address. No personal data is sent with this request. This feature can be disabled in Settings.

No other network requests are made. All network information displayed by the app (IP addresses, adapter details, scan results) is read from your local system and is never transmitted anywhere.

Location Access

Quicker IP Pro may request location permission solely to display your Wi-Fi network name (SSID), as required by macOS. Location data is not stored, transmitted, or used for any other purpose. This feature is optional and can be disabled in Settings.

Local Storage

Our apps store preferences and settings locally on your Mac using standard macOS mechanisms (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect any data from any user, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@bskapps.com.
